Privacy policy

Claude Personal is used only by its owner, Anton Kudlay, with his own Google accounts.

  • Data accessed through Google APIs (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts) is processed only on the owner's own computer, for the owner's personal use.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and in the owner's password manager. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
